Miotsukushi: Backdoor Channels

Author: Yuzuha Ougi
Company: Seven Seas
Official Site


Volume 1
A spy has infiltrated the office of a member of Japan’s House of Representatives. Nagumo, the representative’s secretary, hires the genius hacker Mio to identify the spy. Mio completes the job in record time, but he doesn’t want money for a job well done. He wants Nagumo’s body. So begins a relationship between a single-mindedly devoted hacker and a brutally ambitious political operative.
